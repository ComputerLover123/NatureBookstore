Nature and Books is a library where you can find any kind of books you like. Located in the city of Crestfall Valley next to a waterfall. Since 1915 our modern facilities offer many genre of books, ranging from science-fiction to comedy and passing through horror and history. Nature and books also provides incredible reading spots for our passionate readers depending on the types of books they like, wheither it is a calm area next to a flowing river or a cafe with an amazing view. Nature and Books goal is to share the knowledge that is hidden in the many books around and to encourage people to enjoy the nature around them. If you like nature and if reading is one of your hobbies then Nature and Books is the place for you!
